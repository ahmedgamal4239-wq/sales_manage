---
name: First manager access
description: Why privileged access begins through a one-time operator-controlled setup rather than imported staff or a public registration flow.
---

The first higher-management account must be created through an operator-controlled, one-time setup using a chosen employee ID and temporary password. Spreadsheet employee rows remain inactive and role-pending until explicitly configured.

**Why:** The supplied employee workbook has names and IDs but no trustworthy role, branch, or password assignments. Inferring authority or exposing an unauthenticated bootstrap route would grant access without an approved identity decision.

**How to apply:** When expanding onboarding or deployment, keep first-account setup distinct from normal public login. Require a deliberate administrator choice for roles, assignment, and activation; do not promote imported rows automatically.
